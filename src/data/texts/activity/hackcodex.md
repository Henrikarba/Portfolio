At HackCodeX in Riga, my team and I chose to participate in Emergn's challenge, which was dedicated to improving the quality of life for people with disabilities. We were tasked with creating an app or solution that leveraged ChatGPT or other technologies to solve this issue. The event, a weekend-long experience, was exciting and chaotic at times, especially as we raced to get our introductory video ready before the deadline. Ultimately, our team won the challenge.
