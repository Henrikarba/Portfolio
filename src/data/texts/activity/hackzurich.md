At HackZurich 2023, I arrived without a team but quickly connected with others to form a spontaneous group. Without a clear project idea at the start, we brainstormed, collaborated, and worked tirelessly on our project over the course of the event. To our surprise, we secured second place, an unexpected but rewarding outcome that highlighted the power of teamwork and creative problem-solving under pressure. Our success was a testament to the synergy of different perspectives and the drive to push through challenges in real time.
