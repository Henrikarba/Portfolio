From December 3-5, 2025, I participated in QEst Hack 2025, Estonia's first hackathon fully dedicated to quantum computing. I joined Team Enefit to work on an EV fleet charging optimization challenge and contributed mainly through programming and quantum-focused technical guidance. After the event introduction, I helped explain key quantum concepts to teammates and shaped our approach by arguing that a higher-parameter optimization setup made the quantum case stronger. We then built and presented a solution with many active constraints and parameters, and the jury highlighted that strength as a key reason for our result. Team Enefit won first place.
