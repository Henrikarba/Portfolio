The Ovilon website is a one-page informational site designed to give the company a clear and professional online presence when someone searches for them.

It presents the core company story, ongoing developments, and contact information in a simple layout that works well on both desktop and mobile.

A standout feature is the custom admin panel, which lets the company fully manage the development cards on the site. They can add new cards, remove existing ones, reorder them, and edit the content and images whenever needed without relying on a developer for every change.
